Colette Kress: Thank you. Q4 revenue was a record $22.1 billion, up 22% sequentially and up 265% year-on-year. Growth was driven by both training and inference on our Hopper GPU architecture. Jensen Huang: Accelerating computing and generative AI have hit the tipping point. Demand is surging worldwide across companies, industries, and nations. Our sovereign AI initiatives are opening completely new geographies. Every time we improve performance, we drop cost. That is the magic of accelerated computing.
